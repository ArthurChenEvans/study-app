Switch to the colour-blind safe pair (blue and orange) or back to the warm paper theme to compare, and flip any frame to see the current screen in the same spot.

What's left to weigh against your design rules

  • Green and red now mean two things. Your rules keep them for system success and errors. In the standard palette a wrong answer and a destructive action share red. Every state still has an icon and a word, guessed is still hatched, and the colour-blind safe switch brings back your blue and orange pair.
  • The general theme is Gleam's. Faff pink fills the main button with dark text on it, the page is aged plastic yellow in light and underwater blue in dark, and unnamed blue marks neutral info. Pink is too pale to read as text on the yellow page, so links, tabs and chart lines in light mode use a darker pink that isn't in Gleam's palette.
  • Type. Nunito at weights 700 to 900 replaces IBM Plex Sans at 400 and 600, with uppercase button and nav labels. Plex Mono stays for key hints and maths.
  • Shape. 12 to 20px radii, 2px borders and the pressed-down ledge under buttons all go beyond your current radius and "one shadow level" tokens.
  • Density. Rows are taller, so long lists show fewer items per screen. History fits 7 attempts where the current table fits about 10.
  • Amber does more work. It now marks everything due (badges, chips, the due coin), so it needs to stay off anything decorative.
